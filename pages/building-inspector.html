Frequently Asked Questions

What does your building inspection service cover?

Condition assessment, scope verification, and documentation supporting restoration and construction planning.

What is scope verification?

It is confirming that a proposed scope of work matches what the property actually requires. It is useful when a scope has been prepared and the owner wants the property conditions independently assessed against it.

Is this the same as a home inspection for a purchase?

Our inspection work supports restoration and construction planning. If you need an inspection for a specific purpose, call (870) 935-6019 and we will tell you whether what we do fits your situation.

What do I receive from an inspection?

Documentation of the property conditions found. That record belongs to the property owner and can be used as they see fit, including when filing a claim on their own behalf.

Why does an inspection matter before a restoration project?

Because scope written without a proper look at the property tends to miss things. Assessing conditions first means the plan reflects the building rather than an assumption about it.

Do you inspect commercial buildings?

Yes. We assess residential and commercial properties throughout Northeast Arkansas.
