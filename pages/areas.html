Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does Crain Bros Inc serve?

We serve 14 cities across Northeast Arkansas: Jonesboro, Paragould, Blytheville, Gosnell, Bay, Bono, Brookland, Caraway, Harrisburg, Lake City, Monette, Newport, Trumann, and Walnut Ridge.

Do you serve the whole city or only part of it?

The whole city. Each city page covers the entire community rather than a single neighborhood or ZIP code.

Are you available outside normal business hours?

Yes. We are open 24 hours a day and respond to property emergencies throughout our service area.

What if my city is not listed here?

We are based in Jonesboro and have served Northeast Arkansas since 1984. If your property is near one of the areas listed, call (870) 935-6019 and we will tell you whether we can help.

Do you work on both residential and commercial properties?

Yes. Our work covers residential and commercial property, including restoration, repairs, remodeling, and new construction.

Do you subcontract work in outlying areas?

No. We self-perform our trades everywhere we work, which matters most in smaller communities where coordinating several separate companies is difficult.
