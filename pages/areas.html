What We Do in These Areas

Crain Bros Inc has served Northeast Arkansas since 1984. We provide water damage restoration, fire damage restoration, building restoration, construction, remodeling, plumbing, waterproofing, building inspection services, and building consulting throughout every area listed above.

We work on properties that are involved in insurance claims. We document the damage, the property conditions, and the project work, and that documentation goes to the property owner so they can file their claim more effectively on their own behalf.
