A Family Company Since 1984

Crain Bros Inc is a property damage restoration and construction company based in Jonesboro, Arkansas. We have worked on properties across Northeast Arkansas since 1984, and we are still family owned and operated.

Over that time we have handled water damage, fire and smoke damage, storm damage, structural repair, remodeling, plumbing, waterproofing, and full reconstruction. The work ranges from a single damaged room to a property that has to be rebuilt.

Documentation Comes First

What separates a well-run restoration project from a poorly run one is usually the record keeping. Before work begins, we document the property as we find it. As the project progresses, we document what is uncovered, what is repaired, and what is replaced.

That record matters to the property owner long after the work is finished. It shows what the condition actually was, what was done about it, and why.

Damaged Property and Insurance Claims

Crain Bros Inc works on properties that are involved in insurance claims.

We document the damage, the property conditions, and the project work.

That documentation goes to the property owner, so they can file their claim more effectively on their own behalf.

Available 24 Hours

Crain Bros Inc operates 24 hours a day and provides emergency response services. Water damage in particular gets worse by the hour, and the difference between a same-night response and a next-week response is often the difference between drying a structure and replacing it.
