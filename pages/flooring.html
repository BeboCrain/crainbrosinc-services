What Happens Before Flooring Installation

The first visit is used to inspect the room and the current floor. If removal is needed, the exposed surface can show conditions that were hidden before. Loose panels may need fastening. Soft areas may need replacement. Concrete may need patching or surface preparation.

Moisture deserves attention before the new material is installed. A plumbing leak can wet more than the visible flooring. Crawlspace conditions or slab moisture can also affect the floor above. Covering an unresolved problem can damage the replacement material.

An uneven floor may point to a condition below the finish. The guide Why Is My Floor Uneven? explains when the problem may begin beneath the visible floor. Framing and foundation conditions may matter. Moisture or drainage can also play a role. Crain Bros Inc also offers Building Inspection & Diagnostics when the cause is not clear.

Hardwood and Engineered Wood Flooring Installation

Crain Bros Inc offers solid hardwood and engineered wood flooring installation throughout Northeast Arkansas. Solid hardwood has the depth and variation of natural wood. Engineered wood has a real wood surface over a layered core. Both are available in different plank widths and finishes.

Wood flooring responds to moisture and changes inside the building. The supporting floor and interior conditions are reviewed before an installation method is selected. Depending on the product and the property, the floor may be nailed, glued, or installed as a floating system.

The National Wood Flooring Association technical guidelines explain moisture testing, subfloors, installation methods, care, and repairs.

Ceramic, Porcelain, and Natural Stone Tile Flooring Installation

Crain Bros Inc installs ceramic and porcelain tile flooring in homes and commercial properties. Wood-look tile and natural stone are also available for suitable rooms.

The layout is planned before the first tile is set. The shape of the room affects the pattern. Doorways and cabinets affect where the layout begins. Fixtures and adjoining floors also need to be considered.

Tile is unforgiving. If the floor moves or is not flat, grout can crack and tiles can loosen. The supporting floor is checked before installation. Damaged subflooring or uneven concrete can then be addressed as part of the agreed work.

Vinyl Plank Flooring Installation

Crain Bros Inc installs vinyl plank flooring in homes, rental properties, commercial interiors, remodels, and damage rebuilds.

Vinyl plank is often selected for rooms where spills and routine cleaning are expected. Products differ in appearance and construction. The wear layer and installation system can also vary. The room and the supporting floor should guide the choice.

Vinyl plank follows the surface beneath it. High areas can show through. Poorly repaired sections can affect the finished appearance. Movement can also cause trouble at the plank joints. These conditions are checked before installation begins.

Plank direction is planned around the room and its main sight lines. Doorways and nearby floors are considered before the first row is installed.

Vinyl Tile, Sheet Vinyl, and VCT Flooring Installation

Crain Bros Inc performs vinyl tile, sheet vinyl, and VCT flooring installation for residential and commercial properties.

Vinyl tile is installed as individual pieces. This allows different layouts and can make a future repair easier when matching material remains available.

Sheet vinyl creates a more continuous floor with fewer joints. Accurate measurements matter. Careful fitting is needed around cabinets and fixtures. Uneven areas underneath can show through the finished material.

VCT remains useful in some commercial and utility spaces. The supporting floor has to be prepared properly. Accurate tile alignment matters during installation. The finished floor also needs the maintenance system required by the selected product.

The Resilient Floor Covering Institute moisture guidance explains concrete moisture testing and preparation for resilient flooring.

Carpet and Pad Flooring Installation

Crain Bros Inc installs carpet and pad in bedrooms, living areas, offices, hallways, and commercial interiors.

Carpet creates a softer walking surface and can reduce sound inside a room. The material should suit the expected traffic and cleaning needs. The pad must also be appropriate for the carpet and the room.

Existing carpet and pad are removed when replacement is required. Tack strip and protruding fasteners are addressed before the new material covers the floor. Damaged areas and leftover adhesive also need attention.

Seam locations are planned around the room dimensions and available carpet width. Door clearances and floor registers are checked. Transitions are completed where carpet meets another flooring material.

The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency carpet guidance discusses durability, installation, adhesives, cleaning, and maintenance.

Stained Concrete and Cement Floor Finishing

Crain Bros Inc transforms suitable concrete floors with stained finishes in homes, commercial properties, utility areas, and remodeling projects.

The existing slab becomes the visible floor. Cracks and earlier repairs can influence the result. Previous coatings or adhesive residue may also affect the appearance. Differences in porosity can cause the stain to take differently across the room.

The slab is inspected before a finish is selected. This gives the property owner a better understanding of what can be improved and what may remain visible.

Flooring Repair or Replacement

Crain Bros Inc repairs existing flooring when a localized repair can produce a dependable result.

A damaged board or tile may be replaceable when suitable material is available. Loose flooring may be secured after the cause is identified. Failed transitions and damaged trim can also be corrected without replacing the entire room.

Matching is often the deciding issue. Older flooring changes through use and sunlight. Cleaning and normal wear alter its appearance too. A new piece may stand out even when the original product can still be purchased.

The property owner can compare a small repair with a larger replacement area. A deliberate transition may look better when an exact match is unavailable. Full-room replacement may make more sense when the damage is widespread.

Flooring Restoration After Water or Fire Damage

Crain Bros Inc restores flooring affected by water, fire, smoke, impact, and other property damage.

Water-damaged flooring can trap moisture against the layers below it. Moisture may also reach trim or cabinets. Walls and framing can be affected as well. The source must be stopped before repairs begin. The materials beneath the floor must be ready before replacement flooring covers them.

What Should I Do After a Water Heater Leak? explains the first steps after a sudden release. Water Damage Restoration covers removal and controlled drying. It also addresses moisture documentation and preparation for reconstruction.

Fire-damaged rooms may require flooring removal because of direct burning or heat. Smoke residue can affect the surface. Suppression water and debris may add to the damage. Flooring may also need to be opened so repairs beneath it can be completed. Fire Damage Restoration addresses those conditions before reconstruction begins.

Frequently Asked Questions About Flooring Installation and Repair

What types of flooring can be installed?

Crain Bros Inc installs hardwood and engineered wood. Tile options include ceramic and porcelain. Natural stone and vinyl products are also available. Carpet and pad can be installed in suitable rooms.

How do I choose the right flooring for my home?

The room should guide the choice. Water exposure and cleaning needs matter. The floor underneath matters too. Crain Bros Inc reviews those conditions before recommending an installation approach.

Can new flooring be installed over an existing floor?

Sometimes. The existing floor must be secure and suitable for the replacement material. Finished height and door clearance must also be considered.

Does the subfloor need repair before flooring installation?

A loose or damaged subfloor should be corrected before new flooring covers it. Concrete may also need preparation when cracks or old adhesive would interfere with installation.

Can damaged flooring be repaired instead of replaced?

Sometimes. A repair may make sense when the damage is limited. The supporting floor must remain sound. Matching material must also be available.

When should water-damaged flooring be replaced?

Replacement should follow correction of the water source. The materials underneath must also be ready to cover. Crain Bros Inc evaluates those conditions before flooring is replaced.

Does flooring installation include removal of the old floor?

It can. Removal depends on the project scope. Once the old material is gone, the exposed surface can be inspected and prepared.

Are baseboards and quarter round included?

They can be included. Crain Bros Inc performs related trim carpentry when the room needs it.
