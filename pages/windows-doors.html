New and Replacement Window Installations

Crain Bros Inc installs new and replacement windows throughout Jonesboro and Northeast Arkansas. A window project starts with the existing opening, how the window needs to operate, the amount of daylight and ventilation wanted, and the interior and exterior materials that meet the frame.

Before ordering, we measure the opening and inspect the frame, sill, casing, exterior trim, siding, masonry veneer, and nearby finishes. Those conditions help determine whether the project is suited to an insert replacement, a full-frame replacement, a resized opening, or a new opening constructed for the selected window.

After the existing unit is removed, the exposed opening can be checked for deteriorated wood, movement, unsuitable framing, missing flashing, and hidden water damage. Necessary repairs are completed before the new window is set, fastened, insulated, air sealed, and flashed. Exterior cladding and trim are then restored, interior casing is completed, and painted or stained surfaces are prepared for their final finish.

Choosing Window Styles, Frames, and Glass

Window selection affects more than appearance. Operating style influences ventilation, cleaning access, furniture placement, screen location, and how much of the view remains open. Common choices include single-hung, double-hung, casement, awning, sliding, fixed and picture windows, bay and bow units, transoms, specialty shapes, and grouped assemblies.

Frame choices may include vinyl, wood, fiberglass, aluminum, clad wood, and combination products. Glass options may include clear, obscure, frosted, tinted, decorative, tempered, laminated, double-pane, triple-pane, low-emissivity coatings, gas-filled insulating spaces, and grille patterns. We compare products using the opening, the property, manufacturer availability, maintenance expectations, energy ratings, appearance, and the property owner's budget. ENERGY STAR window and door guidance explains climate-zone certification and NFRC performance labels.

Garage-Door Installations and Operating Systems

Window and Door Repairs and Restoration

Crain Bros Inc repairs and restores existing windows and doors in Jonesboro, Paragould, Brookland, Walnut Ridge, Harrisburg, and surrounding Northeast Arkansas communities. Some problems can be corrected without replacing the complete unit. Other conditions require repair of the opening or surrounding wall materials before the window or door can work properly.

Repair work can address broken glass, failed insulated-glass seals, fogging between panes, water leaks, drafts, damaged flashing, deteriorated sealants, worn weatherstripping, rotted wood, damaged frames, jambs, sills, casing and trim, sash balances, locks, handles, hinges, latches, patio-door tracks and rollers, thresholds, sweeps, storm damage, impact damage, water-damaged framing, and garage-door panels, seals, tracks, rollers, hardware, and operators.

A leaking window does not always mean the glass or window unit is the source. Water may enter through failed flashing, deteriorated trim, gaps at siding or masonry, or damage inside the rough opening. Fogging between panes commonly points to a failed insulated-glass seal. A window or door that sticks may be affected by hardware, paint buildup, moisture damage, frame movement, or a changing opening. The repair begins by finding the condition causing the symptom.

Our guide to sticking doors explains how door movement can relate to the door, frame, moisture, and surrounding building. The broader Windows, Doors & Exterior Seals maintenance guide helps property owners observe seals, thresholds, trim, finishes, water staining, and unwanted air movement.

Energy Efficiency, Comfort, Natural Light, and Condensation

Window and door performance depends on the product and the installation. Frame and glass ratings affect heat transfer and interior surface temperature. Air leakage can occur through the unit, around the perimeter, or through the surrounding opening. Natural light can improve a room, while orientation, shading, and solar heat gain affect comfort differently throughout the day and year.

Condensation on the room-side surface is influenced by glass temperature, indoor humidity, outdoor temperature, and air circulation. Moisture trapped between panes is a different condition and can indicate failure of the insulated-glass seal. The U.S. Department of Energy's window guide explains that even an efficient window can be drafty or develop condensation when installation is poor.

Crain Bros Inc can complete the connected framing, flashing, insulation, siding and exterior cladding, masonry veneer, shutters, interior casing and exterior trim, drywall, painting and staining, and finish carpentry needed to complete the opening.

Frequently Asked Questions About Window and Door Work

Do you install new and replacement windows?

Yes. Crain Bros Inc installs windows in new openings and replaces existing windows using the installation approach appropriate for the opening and surrounding construction.

Can an opening be repaired before installation?

Yes. Damaged framing, sills, trim, flashing, insulation, siding, masonry, and interior finishes can be addressed as part of the window or door project.

Can a window or door be repaired instead of replaced?

Some glass, seal, hardware, balance, track, threshold, jamb, trim, and flashing problems can be repaired. The condition of the unit and opening determines whether repair or replacement is appropriate.

Why is there fog between window panes?

Fogging or moisture between panes commonly indicates a failed seal in the insulated-glass assembly. Component availability and the condition of the frame affect the repair decision. The Windows, Doors & Exterior Seals guide explains other seal and moisture conditions property owners can watch for.

Why does water leak around a window?

Water can enter through the window, perimeter seal, flashing, trim, siding, masonry, or rough opening. The source should be found before selecting the repair, and resulting material damage may also require water damage restoration.

Do you install exterior and interior doors?

Yes. The work can include entry doors, secondary exterior doors, interior doors, patio doors, glass doors, sidelights, transoms, jambs, casing and trim carpentry, hardware, painting, and staining.

Do you install garage doors and operators?

Yes. Garage-door work can include the door assembly, tracks, rollers, springs, seals, operator, controls, safety sensors, and related opening framing and exterior finish work.

Can you match the surrounding siding, masonry, and trim?

Crain Bros Inc can complete related siding, masonry veneer, casing, exterior trim, and finish carpentry, and painting and staining. Exact material matches depend on product availability and the existing material.

Do you offer different manufacturers and price ranges?

Crain Bros Inc can work with multiple manufacturers and suppliers. Selection depends on the opening, construction, features, availability, lead time, maintenance needs, and budget.

Do you serve communities outside Jonesboro?

Yes. Window and door installation and repair services are available in Paragould, Brookland, Walnut Ridge, Harrisburg, and other Northeast Arkansas communities.
