What Windows & Doors Covers

Window failures are overwhelmingly installation failures rather than product failures. Flashing, sill pan, and the order in which the weather barrier is lapped determine whether water that gets past the unit drains back out or into the wall.

That water damage is invisible. It happens inside the wall assembly, and by the time staining appears on interior finishes the framing and sheathing around the opening have usually been wet for a long time.

Replacement is also the opportunity to correct an opening. Openings that were never framed square, that lack proper headers, or that were modified without structural consideration can be corrected while they are open.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do windows leak if the window itself is fine?

Because most window leaks are installation details rather than product defects. Flashing, sill pan, and the lapping order of the weather barrier determine whether water drains out or into the wall.

Can an opening be resized during replacement?

Yes, with the framing and header work that requires. Replacement is the right time to correct an opening that was never framed properly.

How do I know if water is getting in around a window?

Often you cannot until it is significant, because it happens inside the wall. Staining on interior finishes usually means the framing around the opening has been wet for a while.

Are new windows worth it for energy alone?

It depends on what you have and how the building performs overall. Windows are one part of an envelope, and replacing them while ignoring insulation and air sealing gives less than expected.

Do you replace interior doors?

Yes, including hanging, casing, and finishing, and including openings that need reframing to fit properly.

Do you self-perform window and door work?

Yes. It is one of the trades our own crews perform.
