Frequently Asked Questions

What does a building consultant do?

Professional consulting for property restoration planning, scope review, and construction coordination. It is advisory work that supports an owner making decisions about their property.

When would I need a consultant rather than a contractor?

When the question is what should be done and in what order, rather than who will perform the work. Scope review and planning frequently come before any construction decision.

What is scope review?

Examining a proposed scope of work against the property conditions to see whether it reflects what the building actually requires.

Can you consult on a project another contractor is performing?

Call (870) 935-6019 to discuss the situation. What is useful depends heavily on where the project stands.

Do you document your findings?

Yes. We document property conditions and project work. That documentation goes to the property owner.
