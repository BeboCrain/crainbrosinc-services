Frequently Asked Questions

What happens first after a fire?

The property needs to be stabilized and secured, and conditions documented before cleanup begins. Call us at (870) 935-6019. We are available 24 hours a day and respond to emergencies.

Why is smoke damage a separate problem from fire damage?

Smoke travels far beyond where anything burned. It moves through ductwork, wall cavities, and into rooms untouched by flame, and it settles into porous materials. Fire damage and smoke damage frequently affect very different parts of a property.

Should I clean up before you arrive?

Do not disturb more than necessary for safety. Conditions after a fire are part of the record of what happened, and cleaning before documentation removes information the property owner may need later.

What does your fire damage work include?

Stabilization, documentation, cleanup planning, and rebuild after fire and smoke damage events. We also document the property conditions and project work for the owner.

Is water damage common after a fire?

Yes, very. Suppression puts a substantial volume of water into a structure, so fire-damaged properties frequently need both fire and water damage work. We handle both.

Do you rebuild, or only clean up?

We do both. Stabilization and cleanup planning through to the rebuild, so one contractor carries the project rather than passing it along.
