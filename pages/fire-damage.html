Fire Damage Restoration

A fire causes three kinds of damage at once. What burned, what the smoke reached, and what the water used to put it out affected. All three have to be addressed or the building is not restored.

Smoke Reaches Further Than Fire

The burned area is obvious. Smoke residue is not. It travels through the structure, settles on surfaces well away from the fire, and works its way into porous materials, cavities, and mechanical systems. A room that never saw flame can still need full cleaning.

Residue is also corrosive and it does not stop acting when the fire is out. The longer it stays on surfaces, the more damage it does and the harder it becomes to remove. That is why cleaning is sequenced early rather than left until the rebuild.

Then there is the water. Suppression puts a considerable volume of water into a structure, and that water behaves exactly like any other water intrusion. It has to be extracted and the structure dried, or a fire project turns into a mold project.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does a room that did not burn need cleaning?

Because smoke travels. Residue settles on surfaces throughout a structure, well beyond where flame reached, and works into porous materials and cavities. Visible fire damage is a poor guide to the actual affected area.

Can smoke odor be sealed in rather than removed?

Sealing addresses the symptom. If the residue producing the odor is still in the material, the odor comes back, usually when humidity rises. Odor is addressed at the source.

Is water damage part of a fire loss?

Almost always. Suppression puts a large volume of water into the building, and that water behaves like any other intrusion. It gets extracted and the structure dried under monitoring.

How soon does cleaning need to start?

Soon. Smoke residue is corrosive and keeps damaging surfaces after the fire is out. Delay makes removal harder and increases what has to be replaced rather than cleaned.

What happens to belongings?

Contents are handled on their own track. They are inventoried, cleaned, deodorized, and stored while the structure is being repaired.

Do you handle the rebuild as well?

Yes. We self-perform the trades, so the same company that cleaned and documented the property rebuilds it.
