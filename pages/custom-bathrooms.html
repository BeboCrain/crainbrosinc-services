A Local Bathroom Remodeling Contractor Since 1984

A bathroom may be one of the smaller rooms in a home, but the room still has to work hard every day. A good remodeling plan keeps water where it belongs, helps moisture leave the room, provides useful light and storage, and gives people enough space to move comfortably. Those practical details also make the finished bathroom easier to clean and live with.

Crain Bros Inc approaches bathroom remodeling as a complete construction project. We look beyond the visible tile and fixtures to the framing, plumbing, electrical work, ventilation, waterproofing, floor preparation, cabinetry, countertops, drywall, trim, and painting that support the finished room.

Whether you want a focused update or a complete redesign, we help turn practical needs into a room that feels comfortable, works better every day, and looks finished from wall to wall.

Plan the Whole Bathroom Before Work Begins

Good bathroom planning starts with the way the room needs to work. The shower or tub, vanity, toilet, doors, windows, storage, lighting, ventilation, and walking paths should be considered together before finishes are selected.

Layout and Comfortable Movement

A workable plan leaves useful space around the vanity, shower, tub, toilet, doors, and storage. Early planning also helps determine which plumbing, electrical, framing, and flooring changes belong in the project.

Connected Features and Finishes

The room should feel like one bathroom rather than a collection of separate purchases. We help homeowners choose finishes that work well together and make the important decisions early, before installation leaves fewer practical options.

Storage That Fits Daily Use

Useful storage should make the morning routine easier without taking over the room. We tailor the vanity and built-in storage around what the homeowners need close at hand and how much open space keeps the bathroom comfortable.

A Custom Shower Is More Than Tile and Glass

A custom shower should feel comfortable to enter, simple to use, and natural within the room. The work behind the tile matters just as much as the visible finish, so we plan how the shower drains, how the waterproofing protects the surrounding structure, and how the completed entry fits the people who will use it.

Convenience depends on putting each feature where it is comfortable to reach and use. That is why we work through seating, storage, water controls, and the shower entry while the walls are open. Planning those details early gives homeowners a shower tailored to daily routines and future needs.

Decisions Worth Making Before Materials Are Ordered

Keep the Layout or Change It?

Keeping a workable layout may reduce disruption. Moving fixtures can improve the room, but it can also affect drains, supplies, framing, electrical work, flooring, and schedule.

Shower, Tub, or Both?

Choose around the way the household uses the room. Entry height, bathing preference, available space, controls, storage, cleaning, and future needs all matter.

Plan for Easier Use

A wider entry, comfortable clearances, helpful lighting, reachable controls, backing for future grab bars, a bench, or a lower-threshold shower can make the room easier to use without making it look institutional.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I decide before remodeling a bathroom?

Start with what needs to work better. Consider the shower or tub, vanity, storage, lighting, ventilation, fixture placement, walking space, flooring, cleaning, future needs, and the finished look of the room.

Can an older bathroom be updated without changing the layout?

Yes. If the existing layout still works, the project can focus on the shower or tub area, vanity, fixtures, lighting, ventilation, flooring, wall finishes, trim, and paint.

Can Crain Bros Inc change the bathroom layout?

Layout changes are possible when the project allows for the related plumbing, drainage, framing, electrical, flooring, and finish work. The existing conditions must be evaluated before the new plan is finalized.

Can a custom shower be part of a complete bathroom remodel?

Yes. A custom shower can be planned with the rest of the bathroom so the entry, fixtures, bench, niche, tile, glass, waterproofing, drain, vanity, flooring, and lighting work together.

Why does bathroom ventilation matter?

Ventilation helps remove moisture and odors from the room. Fan capacity, duct routing, exterior termination, controls, and actual room use should be considered as part of the remodel.

Can the bathroom be planned for easier future use?

Yes. The plan can consider wider entries, comfortable clearances, better lighting, reachable controls, backing for future grab bars, seating, and a lower-threshold shower where the room and approved scope allow.

Do you provide bathroom remodeling outside Jonesboro?

Yes. Crain Bros Inc provides bathroom remodeling in Jonesboro and throughout Northeast Arkansas. Homeowners can follow the linked city pages in the opening section to learn more about service in each community.

Who coordinates the connected trades?

Crain Bros Inc coordinates the approved bathroom scope as one managed project, including the connected construction, plumbing, electrical, ventilation, cabinetry, countertop, flooring, drywall, painting, trim, and finish work included in the agreement.
