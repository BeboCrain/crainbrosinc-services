Frequently Asked Questions

What choices matter most when planning a custom shower?

Important choices include shower size, entry style, tile or wall finish, fixture placement, drain location, glass layout, niches, shelves, bench seating, and how the shower connects to the rest of the bathroom.

Can a bathtub be changed into a walk-in shower?

Often it can. The existing bathroom layout, drain location, water lines, floor structure, and available space determine what needs to change.

Can an existing shower opening be enlarged or changed?

Often it can, depending on the surrounding walls, plumbing locations, floor structure, and how the finished bathroom layout needs to work.

Can a custom shower include built-in storage or a bench?

Yes. Niches, shelves, benches, and other storage details can be planned into the shower layout before finish materials are installed.

What makes a custom shower easier to use every day?

A better entry, useful fixture placement, comfortable size, good lighting, planned storage, easy-to-clean surfaces, and a layout that fits the room all improve everyday use.

Can a custom shower be part of a larger bathroom remodel?

Yes. A custom shower can be a focused project or one part of a complete bathroom remodel.
