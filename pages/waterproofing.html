Frequently Asked Questions

What does waterproofing actually prevent?

It addresses water intrusion into a structure, protecting building materials and reducing the likelihood of repeat damage in areas that have taken on water before.

Should I waterproof after water damage, or before?

Both are common. Waterproofing after a loss addresses the route the water took. Waterproofing beforehand is preventive. Either way, it is worth identifying how water is reaching the structure.

What parts of a property usually need waterproofing?

Areas below grade and at ground level are the most common, along with any point where the building meets the ground or where water is directed toward the structure rather than away from it.

Does waterproofing fix an existing drainage problem?

Not by itself. If water is being directed toward the building, that needs addressing as well. We assess the property conditions before recommending an approach.

Do you waterproof commercial buildings?

Yes. We provide waterproofing for residential and commercial properties across Northeast Arkansas.
