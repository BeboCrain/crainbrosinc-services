Property Owners Maintenance Plan - Multi-Part Series

A written maintenance plan helps property owners identify what is installed, record inspections and service, track developing conditions, and schedule follow-up based on the property and products actually in use.

Build Maintenance Around the Actual Property

Useful maintenance planning begins with the property, systems, equipment, and materials actually installed. Records can include manufacturer information, installation dates, warranties, service history, inspection observations, photographs, repairs, replacement dates, unresolved concerns, and the next planned review.

The Home Maintenance Plan now includes four developed guides. Additional installments will be added only after their content and pages are ready for review.

Stopping Leaks Before They Damage Your Home
