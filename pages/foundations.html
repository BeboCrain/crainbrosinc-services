Frequently Asked Questions

What should be considered before foundation work begins?

The structure, loads, soil conditions, drainage, access, grade, concrete needs, and how the work connects to the rest of the project all matter.

Can foundation work be needed for an addition?

Yes. Additions often need footings, pads, piers, or other support work before framing and finish work can begin.

How does drainage affect foundation work?

Water should be directed away from the structure. Drainage, grade, gutters, and site conditions can all affect how foundation work performs over time.

Can foundation work tie into an existing structure?

Yes. When new work connects to an existing building, the foundation work has to be planned around the existing structure and the new load path.

What signs may point to foundation concerns?

Cracks, movement, uneven floors, gaps, settling, drainage problems, or doors and windows that no longer work correctly can point to foundation-related concerns.

Do you handle foundation work across Northeast Arkansas?

Yes. Crain Bros Inc works on foundation-related construction and repair needs across Northeast Arkansas.
