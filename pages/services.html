Services for Homes and Commercial Properties in Northeast Arkansas

Crain Bros Inc provides restoration, repairs, remodeling and construction for homeowners and commercial property owners throughout Northeast Arkansas under our Arkansas general contractor licensing.

Every property owner has different needs, and every property presents different conditions. Crain Bros Inc uses inspection, assessment, diagnosis, solution design, property planning, project planning and documentation to develop a tailored scope of work. We then manage and perform the restoration, repairs, remodeling or construction required to meet the property owner’s needs and improve how the home or commercial building functions, performs and serves the people who use it.

The services on this page are arranged to show how Crain Bros Inc works with a property as a whole. The page then moves through the building and property lifecycle, including inspection, analysis, design and planning; property damage restoration and emergency response; residential and commercial remodeling, renovations, alterations and additions; foundation, drainage, waterproofing and crawlspace work; and the individual trade services used throughout these projects.
