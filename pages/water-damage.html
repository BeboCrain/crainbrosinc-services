Water Damage Containment, Dust Control, and Worksite Protection

Containment protects occupied and unaffected areas while water-damaged materials are removed, cleaned, dried, and repaired. Crain Bros Inc sets up water damage work areas in Jonesboro and surrounding Northeast Arkansas communities according to the property conditions and work being performed. The solution may include controlled entry, floor and travel-path protection, dust barriers, debris control, separation of clean and affected areas, and worker protection appropriate to the identified hazards.

Water damage work can involve unstable materials, electrical hazards, contaminated water, chemicals, heavy wet debris, generators, and demolition. OSHA cleanup-hazard guidance calls for a preliminary worksite inspection, attention to structural stability, electrical and gas hazards, safe generator use, suitable protective clothing, safe lifting, sanitation, and emergency planning. Crain Bros Inc uses the conditions found at the property to determine the dust barriers, controlled access, floor and travel-path protection, debris controls, worker protection, and other worksite safeguards needed before cleanup and reconstruction proceed. When cutting, grinding, drilling, or disturbing concrete, brick, block, mortar, stone, or other silica-containing material could expose workers to respirable crystalline silica, the applicable OSHA construction silica requirements govern exposure controls, restricted work-area access, housekeeping practices, worker training, and the written exposure-control plan. Dust barriers support the worksite protection plan, but the required controls must match the materials, tasks, exposure, and hazards present. The EPA also explains that different water-damaged materials require different responses, including water extraction, dehumidification, drying wall cavities and underflooring, or discarding materials that cannot be restored. Water Damage Restoration FAQs

What should I do first after discovering water damage?

Stop the source if it is safe, avoid electrical hazards, document visible conditions, and request help with emergency water removal and inspection.

Do you provide water cleanup and water extraction?

Yes. Depending on the property, the work may include emergency water extraction, water removal, flood cleanup, affected-material removal, cleaning, containment, structural drying, and repair planning.

Can water damage be hidden behind walls or under floors?

Yes. Water commonly reaches wall cavities, insulation, framing, floor decking, cabinets, ceilings, and mechanical spaces beyond the visible wet area.

How do you decide whether materials can be dried or must be removed?

The decision depends on the material, water exposure, physical condition, moisture readings, contamination concerns, access, and whether the material can be cleaned and dried effectively.

Do you handle repairs after structural drying?

Yes. Crain Bros Inc performs restoration repairs and reconstruction involving building systems and finishes affected by the water damage.

Do you work on properties involved in insurance claims?

Yes. We document damage, property conditions, project scope, work progress, and completed repairs so property owners have clear information they can use on their own behalf.
