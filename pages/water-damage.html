A Dry Surface Is Not a Dry Building

Water travels to the lowest point it can reach and moves along framing, under flooring, and into wall and ceiling cavities. By the time it is discovered, the wet area is routinely larger than the visible one. Surfaces dry first and dry fastest, which is exactly why a dry surface says very little about the material behind it.

Structural drying is a measured process. Materials hold moisture at different rates, so drying is monitored against readings rather than declared finished because a room feels dry or a fan has run for a certain number of days.

What gets left wet inside a wall or under a floor does not stay a water problem. It becomes a material problem, then a mold problem, then a repair that costs considerably more than drying it correctly the first time.

Moisture Mapping & Monitoring

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first after discovering water damage?

Stop the water source if you safely can, and shut off electricity to affected areas if water is near outlets or fixtures. Then call us at (870) 935-6019. Take photos before moving anything if it is safe to do so.

How long does structural drying take?

It depends on how much water there is, which materials absorbed it, and how far it travelled. It takes as long as the readings say it takes. Anyone quoting a fixed number of days before seeing the property is guessing.

Can water damage be hidden behind walls or under floors?

Yes, and it commonly is. Water spreads well beyond the visible wet area, which is why inspection matters. What shows on the surface is frequently smaller than what has actually been affected.

Why monitor moisture instead of just running equipment?

Because equipment placed in a room does not prove the material inside a wall is dry. Monitoring is what separates a building that is dry from a building that appears dry.

Do you dry materials in place or remove them?

Both, depending on the material and how wet it is. The readings decide. Removing more than necessary is its own kind of damage.

Do you handle the repairs after drying?

Yes. We handle inspection, documentation, drying, repairs, and reconstruction with our own crews.
