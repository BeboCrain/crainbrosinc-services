How We Work

Crain Bros Inc begins by understanding what the property owner and occupants need from the building. The project may involve repairing damage, correcting a recurring condition, improving comfort or efficiency, supporting a different occupancy, meeting accessibility needs, or preparing the property for a new purpose. We inspect and analyze the building, apply building science to the conditions and systems involved, and develop a solution that fits the property's use, code requirements, construction, and long-term purpose.

Diagnose the Need

We begin with the need the property owner is trying to solve. That need may involve damage, moisture, structural movement, an inefficient layout, uncomfortable rooms, changing occupancy, accessibility, energy use, maintenance, appearance, or a planned improvement. We inspect the property and analyze both the existing conditions and the desired result before deciding what work belongs in the solution.

Design the Solution

We design the solution around the property and the people who use it. The plan considers how the space should function, how the work affects connected materials and building systems, what codes and permits apply, and what the property owner wants to improve. The result may involve restoration, repairs, remodeling, alterations, construction, or a coordinated combination of services.

Protect the Property Going Forward

When damage or a recurring condition is involved, we address what allowed it to happen instead of covering the visible symptom. For improvement projects, we look ahead at durability, maintenance, comfort, efficiency, indoor air quality, flexibility, and future use. The goal is work that continues serving the property and its occupants after the project is complete.

Perform the Work

Crain Bros Inc carries the approved solution through the field work as the primary contractor responsible for project management and performance. Keeping inspection, design, work sequence, trades, and the completed result under one accountable contractor helps preserve the plan and keeps the property owner from having to coordinate the same need through multiple contractors and agreements.
