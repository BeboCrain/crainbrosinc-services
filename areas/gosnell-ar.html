Serving Gosnell and Mississippi County

Gosnell is a separate incorporated city in Mississippi County, considerably smaller than Blytheville, and predominantly residential. The two cities share delivery ZIP code 72315, which occasionally causes confusion, but they are distinct places with different housing.

Most of what we do in Gosnell is residential: roofing, remodels, water and storm damage restoration, and repair work on homes that have been standing long enough to need it. Smaller cities also tend to have fewer contractors self-performing multiple trades, which is where being able to cover framing, plumbing, electrical, and finish work with our own crews matters.

The city is close enough to the steel corridor that some of the same growth pressure reaches it, particularly in housing.

Crain Bros Inc serves all of Gosnell, Arkansas, covered by ZIP code 72315.

What kind of work do you do in Gosnell?

Mostly residential: roofing, remodels, water and storm damage restoration, and general repair work on established housing.

Do you self-perform trades in Gosnell?

Do you take commercial work in Gosnell?
