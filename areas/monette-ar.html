Serving Monette and Craighead County

Monette is a small agricultural community where most buildings have been standing for a considerable time. The work reflects that: roofing, structural repair, remodeling of established homes, and storm damage restoration.

Storm exposure is a real factor across this part of Northeast Arkansas. Wind and hail damage to roofing and siding is common, and it is frequently underestimated because most of it is not visible from the ground. A roof can be genuinely damaged and still look intact from a driveway.

Monette is covered by ZIP code 72447, and we serve the entire city.

How do I know if my roof was damaged by a storm?

Usually you cannot tell from the ground. Lifted shingles, fractured decking, and damaged flashing are not visible from a driveway, and a roof can be damaged while looking intact.
