Serving Brookland and Craighead County

Brookland has added housing steadily, which shapes the kind of work we do here. Newer homes generate remodels, finish upgrades, additions, and the occasional warranty-era defect that was not caught when the house was built.

Newer construction also produces a specific category of problem: builder-grade details that were adequate at closing and are not adequate under real use. Drainage that was graded for the plan rather than the finished lot, flashing details rushed at the end of a build, and undersized gutters are common findings.
