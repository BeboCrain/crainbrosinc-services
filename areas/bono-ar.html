Serving Bono and Craighead County

Bono has grown as a residential community for people working in Jonesboro, which means a mix of established older homes and newer construction sitting side by side. Those two categories need very different things from a contractor.

Newer homes are usually about remodeling, finishing, and upgrades. Older homes are about what is behind the walls: wiring that was adequate for a different era, plumbing that has reached end of life, and framing details that were standard when built and are not now.
