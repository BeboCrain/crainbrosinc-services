Serving Paragould and Greene County

Paragould is the Greene County seat and sits close enough to Jonesboro that our crews move between the two without the scheduling penalty distance usually creates. That matters most on projects that need daily presence rather than a weekly visit.

The housing stock here spans a wide range of construction eras, and a considerable share of the work we do involves buildings that have been modified more than once over the years. Previous work is not always documented and is not always to code, which is why opening a wall in an older Paragould home occasionally changes a scope.
