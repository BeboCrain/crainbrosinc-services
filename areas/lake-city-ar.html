Restoration, Repairs & Construction in Lake City, Arkansas

Lake City sits east of Jonesboro near the St. Francis River, and water is a genuine consideration in how buildings here are built and repaired.

Call (870) 935-6019

Serving Lake City and Craighead County

Lake City's proximity to the river makes moisture and drainage a more prominent factor than in most of the communities we serve. Crawlspaces, foundations, and drainage details matter more where groundwater is closer to the surface, and a repair that ignores that context will not hold.

That shapes the work. Crawlspace encapsulation, drainage correction, foundation repair, and water damage restoration all show up here at a higher rate than in drier locations, and the diagnosis step matters more because the cause is frequently environmental rather than a single failure.

The city is covered by ZIP code 72437, and we serve all of it.
