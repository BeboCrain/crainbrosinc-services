Serving Walnut Ridge and Lawrence County

Walnut Ridge is a county seat with a downtown of older commercial buildings and residential neighborhoods spanning a wide range of construction eras. The community of College City merged into Walnut Ridge in 2017, and Williams Baptist University is located here.

The building stock reflects a long history, which means most of our work involves structures where the original construction methods matter. Older framing, earlier wiring and plumbing practice, and decades of accumulated modifications all affect what a project actually involves once work begins.

Walnut Ridge is covered by ZIP code 72476, and we serve the entire city.
