Serving Trumann and Poinsett County

Trumann has an industrial history that shows in its building stock. There is established residential housing built for a working population, along with commercial and light industrial structures that have been repurposed more than once over the years.

Buildings that have changed use are a specific kind of project. What was adequate for the original purpose is frequently not adequate for the current one, and the modifications made along the way were not always permitted or documented. Establishing what is actually there comes before scoping anything.
